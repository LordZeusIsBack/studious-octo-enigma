House rules

Six things, then we deal.

  1. i.

    Be on time

    First deal is at 10:45. The table waits for no one, not even the host.

  2. ii.

    Phones face down

    Check them between hands. During a hand, the only screen we want to read is your face.

  3. iii.

    Indoor voices

    It is called Shhhh for a reason. The walls are thin and the neighbours have exams.

  4. iv.

    Chips, not cash

    Nothing real is wagered tonight. Not money, not chores, not your roommate's Maggi.

  5. v.

    Clean as you go

    Cups, wrappers and stray cards leave with you. Someone lives in {{venue}} tomorrow.

  6. vi.

    Can't make it? Say so

    Tell {{host}} so someone else gets your chair.

Questions

Asked, answered.

I've never played. Can I join?

Yes. Pick “Never played” on the form and we'll walk you through the first round. Exploding Kittens takes five minutes to learn. Hold'em takes one hand and the rest of your life.

How is the game picked?

Everyone ranks the three games when they book. A first choice scores 3 points, a second 2, a third 1. Highest total gets dealt. To change your vote, message {{host}}.

How do I cancel?

Message {{host}} at {{hostEmail}}. Your seat goes back on the board for someone else.

Does it cost anything?

No. Entry is free, and if Hold'em wins the vote, we play for chips only.

What should I bring?

Yourself, on time. Snacks and drinks are welcome. Mention them on the form so we don't end up with six packets of the same chips.
